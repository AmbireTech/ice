Ambire Signer

A Safe-owner signing device. Its keys are generated inside two secure elements from two different vendors and never leave them, so there is no seed phrase. It decodes every Safe transaction on its own screen. A physical switch cuts the radio and USB data, and the see-through case shows the whole board.

What actually lost money, 2024–2026
$1.4BBybit, Feb 2025. Signers approved a Safe transaction their hardware wallets could not decode; it was a DELEGATECALL.
~$120MColdcard, Jul 2026. A build bug generated seeds from a weak random-number generator.
$72–93MLedger reseller implant, Sep–Oct 2026. An LTE implant was hidden under the screen; the tell was a visible wire.

None of these needed a lab. Each answer is a design choice: decode the Safe transaction on the device, generate keys inside a certified chip, and make implants visible.

Inside

The board

  • 48 × 85 mm with four layers, and every part sits on the back. It is fully routed: KiCad's design-rule check reports 0 violations and 0 unconnected nets.
  • The back layer carries the parts and the wiring inside each block. Under it are a solid ground layer and a 3.3 V plane. The front layer carries the buses between blocks.
  • The USB data pair to the main chip is routed as a differential pair.
  • The cell and the screen cover the front. The only things on it are the four screw holes and the pads for programming the main chip and the radio.
Architecture
PartChoiceWhy
First keyNXP SE050E2 secure elementsecp256k1 and P-256 keys generated inside, never exported. The PIN retry counter lives in the chip. Public docs, open firmware.
Second keyInfineon OPTIGA Trust M V3An independent P-256 key from a second vendor. The Safe counts the device as one owner that needs both signatures, so breaking one chip yields one key, not the device. JLC has none in stock, so early boards can leave it unfitted.
Main chipSTM32U585Same family as Trezor Safe 5/7, certified to PSA Level 3. Runs the screen, transaction decoding and camera.
RadionRF52840 BLE moduleUntrusted pipe for phone and extension. No Wi-Fi on the board.
QR cameraOV2640, refocused to 15 cmReads animated QR from a phone or laptop screen. A global-shutter sensor is the upgrade path.
TransportsUSB HID · BLE · QRWebHID for the extension, BLE for iOS and Android, animated QR (ERC-4527) when airgapped.
Seed modePlanned firmware on the STM32U585For a seed-phrase wallet that does not rely on a second secure element: the PIN is stretched by about a second of encryption inside the main chip, under a hardware-unique key that software cannot read. Guessing PINs off the device first needs a physical attack on the chip. Not built yet; the speed gets measured at bring-up.
RecoveryThe Safe itselfThe device is a replaceable owner, so losing it means swapping an owner.
Post-quantumLater, as an extra ownerML-DSA-44 signs in about 25 ms on this chip. On-chain verifiers are still unaudited, so it waits.
